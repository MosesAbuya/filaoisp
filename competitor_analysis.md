## 2. 13-Dimension Competitor Deep Dive

### Dimension 1: Title Tag & SERP Strategy
- **Safaricom / Faiba:** Rely heavily on brand-first titles (`Safaricom - Home Fibre Packages`).
- **Our Opportunity:** Lead with high-intent exact keyword matches at the front of `<title>` tags (`Residential Fiber Internet Kenya — Fast Home Wi-Fi | Filao Networks`). Bing strongly favors this syntax.

### Dimension 2: Meta Descriptions & CTR Hooks
- **Competitors:** Most use automated or generic descriptions without clear calls to action.
- **Our Opportunity:** Action-driven descriptions with specific speed metrics ("10Mbps to 1Gbps, 99.9% uptime, zero throttling. Request free quote").

### Dimension 3: Heading Structure & Answer Blocks
- **Competitors:** Weak H1/H2 hierarchies. Minimal use of question-format H2s.
- **Our Opportunity:** Deploy self-contained H2 question blocks targeting Google AI Overviews and Perplexity search answers.

### Dimension 4: Schema & Entity Graph Markup
- **Competitors:** Basic organization schema with few `sameAs` entity links and no `FAQPage` schema on plan pages.
- **Our Opportunity:** Deploy rich `InternetServiceProvider`, `LocalBusiness`, `FAQPage`, and `Service` schema across all service pages to maximize Google Rich Snippet eligibility.

### Dimension 5: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & AI Bot Access
- **Competitors:** Standard `robots.txt` blocking AI user-agents or omitting `llms.txt`.
- **Our Opportunity:** Allow PerplexityBot, GPTBot, and ClaudeBot; maintain structured `llms.txt` at site root for direct LLM citation.

### Dimension 6: Core Web Vitals (CWV)
- Competitors typically suffer layout shift from third-party chat widgets and marketing pixels.
- Filao's lightweight modular architecture provides clean, sub-1.5s LCP potential.
