## What System Architecture Is Optimal for Commercial and Industrial Facilities?

For shopping malls, warehouses, educational campuses, and corporate office parks in Nairobi, video surveillance must scale across dozens or hundreds of camera channels without congesting internal office computer networks.

### 1. Power over Ethernet (PoE) Infrastructure
We implement dedicated Cat6A structured cabling backbones with managed Gigabit PoE+ switches, eliminating the need for high-voltage electrical sockets at camera mounting points and supplying power up to 250 meters away.

### 2. Smart Video Storage & H.265+ Codec Efficiency
Continuous 4K video consumes tremendous digital storage. Filao Networks deploys smart H.265+ video encoding algorithms that compress background static elements while preserving pristine detail on moving objects. This compression reduces hard drive capacity and network bandwidth consumption by up to 75%, allowing a 4-bay NVR with surveillance-grade Western Digital Purple or Seagate SkyHawk drives to retain 30 to 60 days of continuous recording.

### 3. Off-Site Cloud Video Replication & Tamper Resistance
Physical digital video recorders located on-site remain vulnerable to theft during break-ins. Filao Networks configures automated offsite cloud video backup channels, ensuring critical alert clips and snapshot logs are saved off-premises immediately upon any perimeter breach.
