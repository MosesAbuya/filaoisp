# Maintenance Schedule & Ongoing Monitoring Plan: Filao Networks Solutions

**Domain:** `https://filaonetworks.com`  
**Effective Period:** 2026 – 2027  
**Owner:** Technical SEO & Infrastructure Engineering Team  

---

## 1. Weekly Tasks

- **Google Search Console & Bing Webmaster Review:**
  - Inspect indexing coverage and crawl error logs (404s, 500s).
  - Verify zero critical schema or mobile-usability errors in GSC.
  - Review submitted sitemap status in both Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- **Uptime & Core Web Vitals Check:**
  - Monitor LCP (<2.5s) and INP (<200ms) on key conversion pages: `/`, `/services/residential-fiber`, `/company/quote`.
  - Validate SSL certificate expiration (minimum 30 days before renewal).

---

## 2. Monthly Tasks

- **Generative Engine Citation Audit (GEO):**
  - Run the 5 priority queries in `geo_tracking.json` across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews to monitor brand citation frequency.
  - Test `https://filaonetworks.com/llms.txt` to verify crawler accessibility.
- **Top Queries & Impression Optimization:**
  - Review GSC Queries report for impressions ranking between position 8 and 20.
  - Enhance page copy and H3 subheadings with high-impression long-tail variants.
- **Blog Publishing & Internal Linking:**
  - Publish 1 to 2 technical cluster articles expanding on local Nairobi fiber topics.
  - Link new blog articles back to core service hubs with descriptive anchor text.

---

## 3. Quarterly Tasks

- **Substantive Content Refresh (Anti-Fake Freshness):**
  - Update pricing, speed tiers, or coverage areas with verifiable new data.
  - Update `dateModified` in schema only when substantive content additions occur (per Google Dec 2025 Core Update guidelines).
- **Competitor Backlink & SERP Gap Analysis:**
  - Monitor new competitor fiber package releases in Nairobi and adjust meta descriptions accordingly.
- **Broken Link & Redirect Audit:**
  - Run an internal crawler sweep to ensure 0 broken internal links and 0 redirect chains.
